Processing of personal data

We process personal data that is necessary to provide this website, respond to enquiries, enable bookings and offer our services. We do so in accordance with the applicable Swiss data protection law.

Contacting us

If you contact us by email or via the contact form, we process the information you provide, in particular your name, email address, optional phone number, selected property and the content of your message, in order to handle your enquiry and communicate with you.

FormSubmit is used for the technical transmission of the contact form. The data entered in the form is transferred to this service for the purpose of sending the message and is then forwarded to our contact address.

Smoobu booking system

The Smoobu booking system is embedded for availability checks, prices and bookings. When you use the booking area, content from the provider is loaded. In doing so, technically necessary data and information entered by you during the booking process may be processed by Smoobu. The privacy information and terms of any additionally integrated booking or payment providers also apply to booking and, where applicable, payment transactions.

Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as is necessary for the relevant purpose or for as long as statutory retention obligations apply.

Provision of the website

When you access this website, technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time of access, pages accessed, browser information and operating system information may be processed by the hosting service. This processing serves the secure and reliable provision of the website.

External services and content

The website is hosted via GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. Technically necessary data may be processed by GitHub and may also be processed outside Switzerland. GitHub's privacy policy applies to data processing by the provider.

Google Fonts are used to display fonts on the website. Loading these fonts may establish a connection to Google servers, which may involve the technical transmission of your IP address.

Some property images are delivered through external image servers. Loading such images may establish a connection to the respective provider and may involve the transmission of technically necessary connection data.

Tracking and analytics

We do not use our own analytics or tracking services such as Google Analytics or Meta Pixel on this website.

Rights of data subjects

Within the scope of the applicable data protection law, you may in particular request information about the processing of your personal data and request the correction or deletion of inaccurate or no longer required personal data. Requests can be sent to the contact details listed above.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website, the services used or legal requirements change. The version published on this website at the relevant time applies.
